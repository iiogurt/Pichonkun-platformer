# Pichonkun: the world and the story

## Logline

**A tiny water droplet born inside an air conditioner sets out to wash a greedy smog cloud out of
the sky, and saves his town's clean air on the way.**

Short versions for captions:
- *One little drop. One big smog cloud. Let's clean the air!*
- *Even a single drop can start a big change.* (Granny Cloud's line from the ending)

## The world: Breezeville

Breezeville is a small, sunny town where the air was always fresh and sweet. On top of the town hall
hums the **Great Air Conditioner**. It cools every home, and its filters keep the town's air clean.

The adventure follows the path that air and water really take through an air conditioner, so the
world is half fairy tale and half machine:

1. **Outside**: the meadow and the hills around town, then the town streets.
2. **Inside the air conditioner**: the intake duct, the big filter, the cold evaporator coils
   (where Pichonkun was born as condensation), and the drain where the water collects.
3. **The outdoor unit**: the hot compressor that pumps heat out of the house.
4. **The source of the problem**: the old smokestack factory.
5. **The sky**: above the smog, where the final battle happens.

Each place teaches one real idea about clean air and saving energy (see *Eco messages* below).

## The story

### Beginning
One night a greedy cloud of pollution called **Smogmaw** crawls out of the old factory. He clogs
every filter of the Great Air Conditioner with dust, pollen and germs. *"Breezeville will be MINE!"*

In the cold cooling coil of the air conditioner, a tiny droplet of water wakes up: **Pichonkun**.
The air smells awful. *"Achoo! Something is very wrong!"*

High above, **Granny Cloud** sees it all and wakes a little guardian to watch over him: **Angel**, a
golden droplet with wings. Angel can only fly where the air is pure, so the cleaner Pichonkun plays,
the sooner Angel can come to help.

A gust of dirty air blows Pichonkun out into the meadow, and the adventure begins.

### Middle: the journey
- **Morning Meadow.** Pichonkun meets **Leafa**, a leaf, who explains that Smogmaw is spreading dirt
  everywhere and that three golden **air crystals** hide in every place.
- **Pollen Peaks.** Smogmaw's smog has made the flowers go wild and spit pollen. **Buzzby** the bee
  asks for help calming them down.
- **Smog Street.** The town itself is full of smog clouds dropping soot from between the rooftops.
- **The Intake Duct.** Inside the machine, **Professor Mesh**, a retired (and slightly clogged) air
  filter, gives Pichonkun the **Vapor Jump**, a second jump in mid-air.
- **Filter Fortress.** Mesh's old home, the great filter, is now ruled by the **Dust Baron**, a
  dust bunny in a top hat and monocle. Pichonkun washes him away.
- **Frosty Coils.** **Frosty** the ice cube shows Pichonkun the place he was born, and warns him
  about spiky flu viruses that love the cold.
- **Drip Drain.** Nobody cleaned the drain, so the **Mold Queen** grew fat on the dirty water.
  Pichonkun washes her away too.
- **Compressor Core.** In the hot outdoor unit, Pichonkun sets the control panel "the eco way" to open the core.
- **Smokestack Factory.** Smogmaw's birthplace. The big door only opens with the three magic words
  of saving the planet: *Reduce, Reuse, Recycle.*

### End
Above the smog, Pichonkun meets **Granny Cloud**, whose blue sky Smogmaw stole. He climbs the cloud
platforms and faces **Smogmaw** himself.

Pichonkun's cool splash washes the soot out of Smogmaw, and all that is left is a gentle grey cloud.
Instead of gloating, Pichonkun makes him an offer: *"Want to become a rain cloud instead? The
flowers in the meadow are thirsty!"* Smogmaw sniffs: *"...That sounds... nice."*

The Great Air Conditioner hums happily with fresh filters, and Breezeville's air is sweet again.
Pichonkun floats up into the sky to join the endless journey of the water cycle.
*"Remember: clean filters, cool not cold, and plant lots of trees! See you in the next rain!"*

**Themes:** small things can make a big change; clean up instead of fighting; even villains can change.

## Eco messages (one per world)

These appear as an "eco tip" after each level. Each one works on its own as a caption or an end card.

1. Plants clean the air for us. Every tree, bush and flower is a tiny air filter!
2. Pollen allergies? Keep windows closed on windy days and let a clean AC filter catch the pollen.
3. Walking, cycling and public transport mean less smog. Your lungs will thank you!
4. Keep vents and ducts clean and unblocked. Clean ducts move air easily and use less energy.
5. A clean filter lets the AC work less hard. That saves energy AND keeps your air healthy.
6. Set your AC to cool, not cold. Every degree warmer in summer saves energy (around 24–26 °C is great).
7. AC units make water. A clogged drain pan grows mold and bacteria, so it needs cleaning too!
8. Turn the AC off when nobody is home, and close windows while it runs. Don't cool the street!
9. Reduce, Reuse, Recycle! Less waste means fewer factories puffing smoke into our air.
10. Clean air is everyone's job. Save energy, plant trees, and keep those filters clean!

## What the player does (for narration)

- **Run, jump and Vapor-Jump** through 10 levels.
- **Shoot water splashes** to wash enemies clean, or jump on the ones that aren't spiky or slimy.
- **Collect water drops** to grow the splash: rapid, twin, triple and finally a mega splash.
- **Fill the Pure Air meter** by playing cleanly, then **call Angel** to help for a short time.
- **Answer easy riddles** at "Air-Q" terminals and solve lever puzzles to open gates.
- **Find 30 hidden air crystals**, some behind secret walls.
- **Beat three bosses**: Dust Baron, Mold Queen and Smogmaw.
- Earn **ranks, achievements, reward skins** and unlock **hard mode**.

**Who it's for:** made for kids and families, and fun for anyone who likes cute retro platformers.
It's free, runs in any web browser, and works with keyboard or touch.
